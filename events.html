Abstract

In the age of AI, the information technology stack, from user interface to database, is rapidly accelerated through automation. This shift creates a strategic opportunity for the physical sciences. Engineers specializing in solid and fluid dynamics, heat transfer, and complex hardware can now move "up the stack" to become full-stack physical technologists. Today, a practitioner in heat transfer can be a system-level developer who applies AI not just to thermal design, but to the entire cyber-physical system. Indeed, this full-stack approach differentiates system performance and delivers a net positive impact measured in Joules.

In this talk, I will explore the application of AI in complex cyber-physical systems, such as modern data centers, where heat transfer is the ultimate differentiator. In an era of hardware-software co-design, physical technologists must move beyond isolated roles to shape end-to-end solutions, using available energy in Joules as our definitive measure of success. Remaining siloed is no longer viable.

About the speaker

Chandrakant Patel is currently the President of Chandrakant Patel Consulting. He is a former Senior Vice President, Chief Engineer, and Senior Fellow of HP Inc. Recognized internationally for advancing the intersection of computing and physical infrastructure, he pioneered innovations in hard disc drives, interconnect and packaging of chips, thermal management of chips and systems, energy-efficient computing, sustainable data centers, cyber-physical systems, digital manufacturing, and city-scale resource optimization.

Over a career spanning industry, academia, and innovation leadership, Patel has taught engineering for more than 16 years, received 165 patents, and published extensively. Today, he is focused on developing the next generation of engineers capable of integrating physical, cyber, economic, and ecological systems to design resilient and sustainable infrastructure for the AI era.

Patel is an IEEE Life Fellow, a Fellow of the National Academy of Inventors, a Member of the National Academy of Engineering, and an inductee of the Silicon Valley Engineering Hall of Fame.
