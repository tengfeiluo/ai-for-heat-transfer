Advancing heat transfer research and engineering with artificial intelligence

AI for Heat Transfer is a community working group that connects thermal science and engineering with AI across academia, industry, and national laboratories.

Join the group Latest news

Mission

The working group coordinates and grows AI-related activity across the heat transfer community. We build the community, venues, and resources that connect thermal science and engineering with AI — maintaining both fundamentals (new AI methods for heat transfer) and applications (AI tools in research, education, and engineering practice), and serving both researchers who develop new algorithms and those who apply existing AI tools in their work. Because AI is a fast-moving target, the group tracks the frontier and brings it to the community early.

  1. Convene — dedicated venues at heat transfer conferences: an AI track at SHTC 2027, panels, tutorials and short courses, and a teaching & education panel.
  2. Communicate — a quarterly webinar series bringing together domain experts, AI experts, and industry voices, and early communication of emerging AI methods to the community.
  3. Chart — a community roadmap paper on the state of the art and outlook of AI in heat transfer, from emerging tools to the grand-challenge problems AI might unlock.
  4. Build — benchmarks, community datasets and data-sharing guidelines, competitions, and education and student activities that grow an AI-fluent heat transfer community.

Subgroups

Four subgroups carry out the group's activities. Each runs its own meetings and welcomes new contributors.

News

Jul 28, 2026

Working group kicks off at SHTC 2026

The group held its hybrid kickoff meeting in Bellevue, WA, with 35 participants from academia, industry, and national laboratories. The group adopted the name "AI for Heat Transfer," confirmed its Year-1 priorities — a community roadmap paper, a quarterly webinar series, and an AI track for SHTC 2027 — and welcomed 15 new members.

Jul 26, 2026

Special issue: AI and machine learning for thermal science and engineering

The Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer has opened a special issue guest-edited by Guoping Xiong, Tengfei Luo, Ming Hu, Masato Ohnishi, and Jianxun Wang. Submissions are due October 31, 2026. Call for papers →
